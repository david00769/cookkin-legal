CookKin Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

CookKin is a family cookbook app for iPhone and iPad. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. In short: your recipes stay on your devices and in your own iCloud account. We do not collect, sell, or share your personal data, and the app contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking.

Who we are

CookKin is published by CI Design Studio LLC ("we", "us"). For support, see the Support link on the App Store page.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not run servers that receive your recipes, photos, cookbooks, or usage. The app does not include third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking code, and it does not use your data to track you across other apps or websites.

Where your information is kept

  • On your device. Recipes, cookbooks, meal plans, grocery lists, notes, and the photos you add are stored on your iPhone or iPad.
  • In your iCloud account. If you are signed in to iCloud, CookKin syncs your data through Apple's CloudKit, into a private database that belongs to your iCloud account. Apple stores it under the Apple Privacy Policy. We cannot see it.
  • Shared with people you choose. When you share a cookbook or invite household members, the shared items are visible to the people you invited, through iCloud sharing. You can stop sharing at any time.
  • Files you send. When you share a recipe or cookbook as a file, it goes wherever you send it (Messages, Mail, AirDrop, Files). We do not receive a copy.

Importing recipes

  • Photos and the camera. When you import from a photo or the camera, the text is read on your device using Apple's on-device text recognition. The app asks for camera and photo access only to do this, and you can change that in Settings.
  • Web pages and videos. When you paste a link, your device fetches that page or video page directly from the site, as Safari would. That site may log the request under its own privacy policy. The page is processed on your device.
  • On-device intelligence. Where available, recipe structuring uses Apple's on-device models. Your recipe text is not sent to us or to an outside AI service.

Timers and notifications

Cook timers use iOS alarms on your device. Alarm and notification permission is requested only when you start a timer.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and managed by Apple. We do not receive your payment details. Apple tells the app only whether your subscription is active. Manage or cancel it in your Apple Account settings.

Children

CookKin is a general-audience app. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Delete recipes and cookbooks in the app, or delete the app. To remove synced data, open Settings > your Apple Account > iCloud > Manage Account Storage, choose CookKin, and delete its data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and the policy in the app.
