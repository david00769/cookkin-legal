CookKin Support

CookKin is a family cookbook app for iPhone and iPad, published by CI Design Studio LLC.

Contact

Email support@cidesignstudio.com with questions, problems or suggestions. Please mention your device and iOS version.

Subscriptions

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. To cancel or change a plan, open Settings > your Apple Account > Subscriptions on your iPhone or iPad. To restore a purchase on a new device, open CookKin's paywall and tap Restore Purchases. For refunds, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your data

Your recipes stay on your devices and in your own iCloud account. See the Privacy Policy.
